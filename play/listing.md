## Full description (4000 max)
PRESS PLAY ON THE 90s.

REVERENDER VHS turns your Android phone into a glowing VCR on-screen display. Blocky dot-matrix text, a humming CRT tube and spinning 3D wallpapers become your home screen, with everything you use every day built right in.

ONE-TIME PURCHASE. FREE UPDATES FOREVER.
Pay once and it's yours. No subscriptions, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account. Every future update is included.

▶ A HOME SCREEN LIKE A VCR
• A full launcher: your apps in a drawer with four looks (float list, tape shelf, teletext, icon grid)
• Type to find an app, press enter and it starts
• Hold an app for app info or to uninstall it
• Pin favourite apps and put any app or menu on a sideways swipe
• Your own title line plus a scrolling news ticker from any RSS feed
• Hold the clock to open your clock app

▶ CRT TV LOOKS
• Seven TV styles, from a flat studio monitor to a broken VCR
• Fine-tune snow, glitch, scan, scanlines and corner curve with sliders
• The tube powers on when you unlock your phone
• Channel-change effects when you switch menus
• Six spinning 3D wallpapers, pixel fonts, text colours and themes

▶ MUSIC DECK AND RADIO
• Internet radio with song titles that scroll while they play
• Add stations by searching, hold one to remove it (with undo)
• Equalizer, playlist and VFD-style visualizers
• Find free, openly licensed music to play

▶ TAPE DECK
• Watch online videos in their own player, framed like a VHS tape
• Play public-domain films, classic cartoons and openly licensed videos

▶ GAME DECK
• Play your own game files for classic 8-, 16-, 32- and 64-bit era consoles
• On-screen controllers that change to fit each system
• Includes a list of free homebrew games straight from their makers
• No games are included and none are downloaded by the app; play only games you own or that are free

▶ VHS WEB
• A small, quick browser with your own homepage
• Leaving it wipes cookies, history and site data, every time

▶ VHS CHAT AND NOTIFICATIONS (OPTIONAL)
• A notification counter in the title bar that flashes the app name of anything new
• Read and reply to chat messages on one retro screen
• Only if you allow notification access. Everything stays on your phone.

▶ HELP BUILT IN
• A guided help tour on first start (hold SKIP to skip it), available again from SETUP

PRIVACY FIRST
No ads, no tracking, no account, no analytics. Notifications you allow it to read stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

OPEN SOURCE
The emulation is built on open-source libretro cores and the LibretroDroid library (GPLv3). The source code is published. See LICENSES in the app.

Set it as your home app, press PLAY and be kind, rewind.
