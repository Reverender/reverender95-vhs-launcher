REVERENDER VHS Launcher — Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026. App: REVERENDER VHS Launcher (com.reverender.vhslauncher), made by REVERENDER (reverender.com).

In short

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn on notification access, the app reads the notifications on your phone to show how many you have, to briefly show the name of the app a new one came from, and to show messages from your apps (chat, SMS, email and other messaging apps) in VHS CHAT, where you can reply. This happens only on your phone. Notification content is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not kept after the app closes. A reply you type in VHS CHAT is handed to the chat app through Android's own reply action; that app sends it. You can turn notification access off at any time in Android Settings.

Installed apps

As a home screen (launcher), the app lists the apps installed on your phone so you can open them. This list never leaves your phone.

Things you choose to use online

  • Radio: streams are played straight from the radio stations' servers.
  • Searches for videos, music and subtitles are sent to the search service (through reverender.com for some searches) only to return results. We do not log them or link them to you.
  • Videos from YouTube play in YouTube's own embedded player, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Free films and music come from the Internet Archive (archive.org).
  • News ticker: the RSS feed you set is fetched directly from its website.
  • VHS WEB opens the websites you visit. When you leave it, its cookies, history, cache and site data are wiped.

Each of those services may see your IP address, as any website does, under its own privacy policy.

Files and settings

Game files you open and your settings are stored only inside the app on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
